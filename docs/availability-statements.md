# Data availability

The 2025 PLATEAU CityGML models for Chiyoda, Chuo and Minato are available at https://www.geospatial.jp/ckan/dataset/plateau-13101-chiyoda-ku-2025, https://www.geospatial.jp/ckan/dataset/plateau-13102-chuo-ku-2025 and https://www.geospatial.jp/ckan/dataset/plateau-13103-minato-ku-2025. The Tokyo Metropolitan Government 0.50 m ground DEM is available at https://www.geospatial.jp/ckan/dataset/tokyopc-23ku-2024/resource/ff8305fd-a993-4535-80b4-fd6dcb339775; the associated point-cloud and orthophoto resources are listed at https://www.geospatial.jp/ckan/dataset/tokyopc-23ku-2024. JMA composite radar rainfall is obtained from the Kyoto University RISH archive at http://database.rish.kyoto-u.ac.jp/arch/jmadata/data/jma-radar/synthetic/original/. The 2020 Population Census population and geographic boundaries are available through e-Stat at https://www.e-stat.go.jp/gis/statmap-search?page=1&type=1&toukeiCode=00200521. Administrative boundaries and river centerlines are obtained from MLIT National Land Numerical Information at https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/gml/datalist/KsjTmplt-N03-2024.html and https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/gml/datalist/KsjTmplt-W05.html. OpenStreetMap road data are obtained from the Geofabrik Kanto extract at https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/japan/kanto.html, using the October 15, 2026 snapshot. The 2021 road-traffic survey data are available at https://www.mlit.go.jp/road/census/r3/index.html. The 2018 Tokyo Metropolitan Area Person Trip Survey data are available at https://www.tokyo-pt.jp/data/H30 through the provider's download and application procedure at https://www.tokyo-pt.jp/data/01download. Provider-specific access and reuse conditions apply. Exact dataset identifiers, census queries, additional resource URLs and rainfall windows are documented at https://github.com/MichaelChaoLi-cpu/UDT01b/blob/91590530184f3fc4e8c23eb951a0c101171f8bb3/docs/DATA_SOURCES.md.

# Code availability

Code for data acquisition and preprocessing, the modified SFINCS solver, numerical verification, road and resident-access analysis, vehicle assignment, and figure and table generation is available at https://github.com/MichaelChaoLi-cpu/UDT01b. The code snapshot accompanying this manuscript is available at https://github.com/MichaelChaoLi-cpu/UDT01b/tree/91590530184f3fc4e8c23eb951a0c101171f8bb3. The README provides environment setup, data-source links and reproduction instructions. Solver source hashes and the pinned upstream SFINCS version are recorded with the build instructions in the repository.
